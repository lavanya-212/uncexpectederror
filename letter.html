To My Dearest Chinna ❤️

A Letter From My Heart To Yours ♡

Happy Birthday, My Nanna ❤️

❤️ You Became My Whole World

My Nanna, My Everything...

I never knew I could love someone this deeply,

I never knew someone could mean this much to me.

In the beginning, I didn't feel all these emotions,

But slowly, your love, your care and your little efforts

Changed something inside my heart.

Somewhere between your smiles and your hugs,

Your care and your endless love,

I fell for you more than I ever imagined.

And now, I honestly don't know how to live a day without you.

☕ **Our Little Everyday Happiness**

Those everyday meetings at the office,

Our little night coffee dates,

Those beautiful night rides and peaceful beaches,

Laughing together, making memories with our friends,

And enjoying even the smallest moments...

Maybe they look like ordinary days to everyone else,

But to me, they are the most beautiful parts of my life.

You made me realise that happiness doesn't need a special occasion.

Sometimes, all it takes is having you beside me.

🥹 **The Little Things You Do**

Whenever I say I'm hungry, you make sure I eat.

Even if you have just one rupee left,

You would spend it to see me happy and satisfied.

Whenever I feel like going somewhere,

You take me there, sometimes even without me asking.

You buy me ice creams, pamper me

And take care of me like I'm your little princess.

You may think these are just little things,

But these little things make me fall in love with you every single day.

You do so much for me,

You let go of so many things just to see me smile.

And honestly, I feel so lucky to be loved by you.

❤️ **Even Through Our Fights**

Yes, we fight, we argue, we get angry,

Sometimes we misunderstand each other too.

But even after every little fight,

My heart still searches for you.

Because I don't want a life where we never disagree.

I want a life where we never stop choosing each other.

You cannot stay without me,

And I cannot imagine my world without you.

That's how deeply we have become a part of each other's lives.

🌷 **A Promise to Your Heart**

I know you have so many little fears and worries about me.

But my love, please don't be scared.

Don't overthink, don't worry too much.

I'm here with you.

I want to hold your hand through every difficult moment,

Celebrate every little success,

Wipe away your tears and be the reason behind your smiles.

I want more coffees, more night rides,

More beaches, more laughter, more silly fights,

More ice creams, more hugs and countless memories.

I want to watch you achieve every dream you've ever had.

I want to grow with you, laugh with you

And spend the rest of my life creating beautiful memories with you.

💕 **And Finally, My Love...**

You came into my life without me knowing

That one day you would become this important to me.

Now, you're in my every thought,

In every little smile,

In every dream about my future.

I don't know how to explain this love,

But I know one thing:

I don't just want you in my today,

I want you in all my tomorrows.

**You are my happiness, my comfort, my favourite person, my safe place and my everything.**

I love you more than I can ever put into words.

And I miss you more than you will ever know.

**If life gives me a thousand more tomorrows,

I want to spend every single one of them with you.**

Forever yours, my Nanna. ❤️🫂
